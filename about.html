About This Website

Learning business analytics through a practical example.

This website was created for a Business Analytics assignment to practice website tracking using Google Analytics 4 (GA4).

Project Objectives

The website demonstrates a simple two-page structure that can be connected to Google Analytics.

  • Track website visitors.
  • Observe page views and engagement.
  • Review traffic sources and audience information.
  • Practice reading GA4 reports.

Why Business Analytics Matters

Business analytics helps organizations use data to identify patterns, measure performance, understand customers, and make better decisions.
